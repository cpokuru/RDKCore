Core RDK Entertainment Platform

RDK-E is an open, modular and scalable software platform for entertainment devices and streaming ecosystems. It provides a common software foundation for rapid innovation, simplified platform integration, secure content delivery and fleet-scale operations across diverse hardware platforms.

RDKE overview

Why choose RDK-E?

RDK-E standardizes core platform functionality while allowing operators, OEMs, and technology partners to focus on creating differentiated user experiences and services. The platform provides a layered architecture that improves portability, maintainability, and time-to-market.
